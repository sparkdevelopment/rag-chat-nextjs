# Welcome to Nimbus Notes

Nimbus Notes is a fictional note-taking app used as sample content for this
RAG demo. Everything below is made up for testing purposes.

## Pricing

Nimbus Notes offers three plans:

- **Free** — up to 50 notes, no file attachments.
- **Plus** ($4/month) — unlimited notes, up to 1GB of attachments.
- **Team** ($9/user/month) — unlimited notes and attachments, shared
  workspaces, and admin controls.

## Syncing

Notes sync automatically across devices every 30 seconds when connected to
the internet. If you're offline, changes are saved locally and sync once a
connection is restored. Conflicting edits made on two devices while offline
are merged automatically, keeping both versions as separate notes if the
merge can't be resolved cleanly.

## Exporting Notes

You can export a single note or your entire notebook from Settings > Export.
Supported formats are Markdown, PDF, and plain text. Exports containing
attachments are bundled into a `.zip` file.

## Support

For help, email support@nimbusnotes.example. Team plan customers get
priority support with a 4-hour response time on weekdays.
